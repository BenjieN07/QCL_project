# Coverage and weighting findings

The same original two-qubit five-layer circuit improved through continued Adam training, mirrored supervised inputs, and a changed loss weight. No replacement architecture or paper model was introduced. Main training inputs increased from 48 to 96 by adding -t with the correct odd-sine/even-cosine targets. Twelve boundary anchors remained supervised constraints. Three 1,500-update branches all resumed identical update-3,000 weights and optimizer moments. Training loops took about 552 seconds combined on this machine, excluding process imports and later evaluation.

The active checkpoint is mirrored_rebalance, update 4,288. Selection score 0.00109056 was only about 0.3% better than mirrored_training's 0.00109369; do not interpret this small validation difference as a universal preference for weaker boundary weighting. The rebalanced branch's update-4,500 endpoint error exceeded 0.05 and was ineligible. Its selected endpoint error 0.04999637 is almost exactly at the guard. The unchanged-weight mirrored branch has a substantially smaller endpoint error, about 0.03314, and remains available for inspection.

The fresh holdout was first evaluated after committing the selection. See evaluation/REPORT.md for every branch. The selected model reduced interior MAE by about 27% for sine and 22% for cosine. On the 1,001-point grid, sine maximum error declined from 0.049683 to 0.041078; cosine from 0.075378 to 0.054862. The large negative-t cosine error was reduced, leaving the center trough as the largest cosine residual. Parity defects fell from 0.05037/0.06644 to 0.04056/0.04594 (sine oddness/cosine evenness). Mirroring taught the model better symmetry without changing its output equation.

There are costs: right-boundary mean errors worsened, and the left-boundary cosine maximum worsened even though its mean improved. Extra training alone increased the grid cosine maximum to 0.07970; filling the data gap helped beyond that control. Original validation cosine MSE is still 0.001314, above the 0.001 target. These outcomes do not establish a circuit capacity limit, quantum advantage or real-device accuracy.

Pause for inspection here. A future optimization should predeclare stronger endpoint protection and compare against the unchanged-weight mirrored checkpoint, using validation rather than selecting against this newly exposed holdout.
